About CareerXP

We got tired of "up to 90% placement" claims nobody could check.

We flipped the process. You start with the free placement drives we run regularly on our social pages — get selected there, and your placement is complimentary. If you don't get selected, CareerXP takes over: our best cohorts get you interview-ready for your dream role within a promised timeline, backed by a no-questions-asked refund on your placement fee if we don't deliver.

Origin

Built by people who were on the hiring side first.

CareerXP started with a simple observation: most bootcamps optimize for enrollment, not employment. The incentives point the wrong way — sell the course, collect the fee, move on to the next cohort.

We flipped that. Placement isn't an add-on service — it's the product. Curriculum is reverse-engineered from actual hiring-partner job descriptions. Instructors are people who currently sit on the other side of the interview table. And every claim we make about outcomes is backed by a record you can ask to see.

The name says it plainly: Career, built on real XP — experience — not assumptions about what the market wants.

That principle carries through the guarantee itself. Every batch runs free placement drives on our social channels — clear one, and CareerXP doesn't charge you for the placement. Miss it, and we don't walk away: you're matched into the right cohort and worked toward an offer within a fixed timeline, with your placement fee refunded in full, no questions asked, if we miss that deadline.
